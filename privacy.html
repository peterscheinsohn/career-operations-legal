Career Operations Assistant

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Career Operations Assistant is a personal productivity tool used by its owner to organize a job search. It is not a public social network or a service for selling personal data.

Information the assistant may access

When the account owner connects a Google account, the assistant may access Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and Google Docs only to the extent expressly authorized by that account owner. It may also receive basic account-identification information needed for authorization.

How information is used

Authorized information is used only for the owner's requested productivity workflows, such as maintaining a job-search tracker, preparing reminders, and creating or updating documents. Information is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to build profiles about other people.

Sharing

The assistant does not share authorized Google data with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be processed by Google APIs when the owner asks the assistant to use Google services. The assistant does not request Gmail access as part of its intended workflow.

Storage and security

Authorized data and access credentials are kept in the owner's controlled environment and are protected with reasonable technical measures. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The owner can revoke Google access at any time through Google account security settings.

Data retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for the owner's personal workflow or until the owner deletes it. Revoking authorization stops future API access but does not automatically delete copies the owner has already created in Google services.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the assistant's workflow changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the app owner through the support contact displayed on the Google authorization screen.
